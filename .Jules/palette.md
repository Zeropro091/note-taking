## 2024-05-15 - Accessible floating control panels
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in floating UI elements (like the graph controls or note detail overlays) are easily overlooked for accessibility since they are typically absolutely positioned over the main interface. When adding `aria-label`s to these, toggle-style buttons (like Pause/Play or Isolate/Reset) must also implement `aria-pressed` states to correctly convey their current active status to screen readers.
**Action:** Always verify `aria-pressed` or `aria-expanded` is used on icon-only toggle buttons in UI overlays, and ensure close buttons have a visible `focus-visible` ring.
